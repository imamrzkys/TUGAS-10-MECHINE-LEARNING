Tentang Proyek

Project ini bertujuan memprediksi status mahasiswa (Dropout / Enrolled / Graduate) menggunakan model K-Nearest Neighbors.

Dataset: "Predict Students Dropout and Academic Success" (Kaggle).

Metode: KNN, K optimal = 21, akurasi ≈ 71.86%.

Alur training singkat
  1. Preprocessing: encoding, scaling, handling missing values.
  2. Feature selection / engineering.
  3. Train-test split, hyperparameter search (pilih K terbaik).
  4. Final training dan simpan `model.pkl` dan `preprocessor.pkl`.
